# Playtest: controller aiming (M4), plus the sharpness fix

Setup is the same as [m1.md](m1.md) (SteamVR on, `tools\profile.ps1 playtest`, `tools\run.ps1 -Normal`,
sit facing forward). Everything from that list still applies; this adds aim mode.

New since the first session:

- **Sharpness / "scanlines":** the game draws many surfaces as alternating 1-pixel columns (it
  does this on a monitor too; the window's downscale hides it). The eye images are now blended
  horizontally before they reach the headset. The fine vertical lines should be gone. If the
  picture looks too soft, `destripe=0` under `[vr]` turns it off for comparison.
- **Controller aim:** hold right grip (aim mode) and point the right controller. The game's aim
  follows the controller; the world stays still while you aim; Dan's arm and gun are in your
  hand (forearm cut off just behind the hand); a red laser comes out of the barrel; the game's
  crosshair is hidden. Shots go along the laser and land exactly on it about 10 m away
  (`aim_converge_m`); nearer or farther they land a little off it (your hand isn't at your eye).

- **The silhouette screen** (the shot that starts a target): point the right controller at the
  silhouette; the red dot goes where you point and a laser runs from the controller to it.
  Right trigger fires. The game's own flat red line is hidden.

What to look at — the silhouette screen first:

- [ ] Does the dot sit where the laser points, and does a shot on the silhouette start the
      target (a miss does nothing, as in the game)?

Then the first running section and the lobby (no enemies there, so aim at walls, doors, the
lobby's lights):

- [ ] Fine vertical lines gone? Sharp enough?
- [ ] Aim mode: does the laser point where the controller points, the way you hold it? If it
      points too high or low, say roughly how many degrees (`hand_pitch`).
- [ ] Is the gun in your hand: position (`hand_offset`, metres in the controller's frame),
      size (`gun_scale`, now 0.6), and does any forearm or sleeve show (`gun_clip_z`)?
- [ ] Turning the controller far left/right/up/down: the world must not swing. Aim up to 80°
      up/down now works (the game allowed 36°).
- [ ] Fire (right trigger) at something: do hit sparks/marks land on the laser? (Headless they
      did, on a wall and on the floor.) The gun kicks up with the game's own recoil animation;
      say whether that feels right in the hand or is too much.
- [ ] Anything left of the crosshair or other HUD you miss in aim mode (`aim_hud_mask`, the
      hidden centre band as a fraction of the HUD width; 0 shows everything).

Settings live under `[vr]` in `C:\Users\Virtual\killer7-vr-game\k7vr\k7vr.ini`:

| key | default | what |
|---|---|---|
| `destripe` | 1 | blend the alternating columns |
| `hand_pitch` | 0 | degrees the aim is tilted up from the controller's pointing axis |
| `hand_offset` | 0,0,0 | grip point in the controller's frame (x right, y up, z back), metres |
| `gun_scale` | 0.6 | gun and hand size |
| `gun_clip_z` | -1.5 | where the forearm is cut (game units; nearer 0 shows more arm) |
| `aim_converge_m` | 10 | distance where shots meet the laser |
| `laser_m` | 30 | laser length (0 = no laser) |
| `aim_hud_mask` | 0.6 | hidden HUD centre band in aim mode |
| `aim_pitch_limit` | 80 | the game's aim pitch limit, degrees |
