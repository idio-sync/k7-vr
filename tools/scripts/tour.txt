# The screen tour (tools\check.ps1): from launch through the first target's opening, every
# screen reached gets `screen NAME` (a pair in the simulated headset, a screenshot with VR off),
# and most get an `expect` first (checked over 1 s, before the capture). The timings are the
# ones the run was worked out with; inserting steps that take time shifts what follows.
# Keyboard works until the pad is used (the first `press A` after the moon); after that only
# the pad does. Pad: A walk/accept, B turn/back, X map, BACK quit prompt, RB camera, RT aim,
# LT eye view (with RT: scan), START pause menu. Pause menu: hold the stick towards an entry,
# A while holding (plain presses are ignored there).
# 3D screens also get a `sweep` (looking around, ~2.9 s); in timed scenes the next wait is
# shortened by 2900 ms to keep later captures where they were.
